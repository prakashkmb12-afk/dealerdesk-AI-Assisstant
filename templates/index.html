Model Methodology & Validation
Demand projections use a trained HistGradientBoostingRegressor evaluating 104 weeks of historical sales. Features include 1-to-4 week demand lags, 4-week and 8-week rolling means, promotional calendar uplifts, and intermittent demand smoothing. Validation was conducted across 4 rolling-origin backtest folds, achieving a 20.64% WAPE (substantially beating the 26.8% moving average baseline).
